Setting Your Availability in Veras

A five-minute walkthrough on your phone. Do it once a month.

📅

Due before the 1st of every month. Enter your availability for the next month by the 1st. This is how the ADON knows which days she can count on you — especially when covering call-offs and vacations.

The one thing to remember: in Veras, an empty day means “yes, I can work.”

You are only marking the exceptions — the days you can’t work, and the times you’d rather work. Every day you leave blank is treated as a day you’re available to be offered a shift.

Before you start

  • Have the Veras app installed on your phone and know your login.
  • Have your calendar handy — school, second job, appointments, family plans for next month.
  • Set aside about five minutes. You can go back and change things later.

The five steps

Tap Schedule at the bottom, then the 3 dots at the top right.

1

Open the Schedule menu

Log in to Veras. At the bottom of the screen, tap Schedule.

Then, in the very top right corner, tap the ⋮ three dots.

Looking in the wrong place?

The three dots sit to the left of the little calendar icon and the person icon. They’re small — look for three dots stacked vertically.

Under Advanced Options, tap Manage Schedule Preferences.

2

Choose “Manage Schedule Preferences”

A menu slides up from the bottom called Advanced Options. Tap the first line: Manage Schedule Preferences.

Ignore Sync with calendar and View everyone’s schedule — those are different tools and won’t save your availability.

Scroll to next month and tap a date.

3

Tap the date you want to mark

You’ll see a calendar. Scroll down to next month — that’s the month you’re filling out.

Tap any date you need to say something about: a day you can’t work, or a day you have a preferred shift time.

You don’t have to touch every day

Only tap the days that are exceptions. If your month is wide open except for two Saturdays, you only tap those two Saturdays.

Tap + Add Preference.

4

Tap “+ Add Preference”

A small panel opens with the date at the top — for example, Sep 4 Preferences. Tap + Add Preference.

Heads up — you will tap “Add Preference” twice

This first tap just opens the form. Nothing is saved yet. You’ll tap a second, purple Add Preference button at the end of Step 5 to actually save it.

Pick Preferred or Unavailable, set the times, then tap the purple Add Preference.

5

Fill in the form and save

A form slides up showing the date you picked. There are three things to set:

Preferred / Unavailable The two buttons at the top. This is the most important choice on the screen. See below.
Starts & Ends The window of time this applies to. For a whole day, set the times to cover the entire day.
Repeat Leave this off for a one-time date. Turn it on when the same thing is true again and again, and the app will let you set how often it repeats.

When it looks right, tap the purple Add Preference button to save.

Don’t mix these two up

Picking the wrong button tells the scheduler the opposite of what you meant — and you may get put on a day you can’t work.

✖  Unavailable

“Please do not schedule me.”

  • You have class, a second job, or an appointment
  • You’re out of town
  • Any day you truly cannot work

✔  Preferred

“I’d like these hours.”

  • You can work, and these are your best hours
  • Example: days only, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM
  • The facility will try to match it, but it isn’t a guarantee
Repeat the steps for each date

Close the form and tap the next date you need to mark. Work your way through the whole month before you close the app.

Check your work before you close the app

Go back to the Schedule preferences calendar and look at next month. The dates you marked should now be highlighted. If a date you meant to block still looks plain, it didn’t save — tap it and try Step 4 again.

  • Every day I can’t work next month is marked Unavailable.
  • My preferred shift times are entered on the days I can work.
  • I left at least one weekend day open — that’s required for PRN status.
  • I understand every blank day means I’m available for a shift offer.
  • I finished this before the 1st of the month.

What your PRN position requires

From the Per-Diem / On-Call Employee Active Status Policy (HR-PRD-001):

WhatHow often
Update your availability in VerasBefore the 1st of every month
Work at least one shiftEvery calendar month, when shifts are available
Be available for a weekend shiftAt least one per month
Work a holiday shiftAt least one per year, when shifts are available
Respond to a shift offerWithin 4 hours during waking hours
Keep the Veras app installedAlways — open shifts are posted there

Missing the minimums for two months in a row, or declining three shifts in 60 days, can put your per-diem status at risk. Before anything happens, the ADON or HR will reach out to you first. If something in your life has changed, tell them — exceptions can be approved.

Common mistakes

What happensWhy
“I got scheduled on my day off.” The date was set to Preferred instead of Unavailable, or it never saved.
“I entered it but nothing shows up.” The purple Add Preference button at the bottom of the form wasn’t tapped.
“I filled out the wrong month.” The calendar opens on the current month. Scroll down to next month.
“I never get offered shifts.” Too many days blocked, or availability wasn’t submitted by the 1st.
“I used a time-off request instead.” Time-off requests are a different tool. Monthly availability goes in Manage Schedule Preferences.

Need help?

If you get stuck, don’t just leave it blank — a blank month reads as “available every day.” Ask for help and we’ll walk through it with you on your phone.
